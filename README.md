## 🏗️ Featured Architectures & Production Systems

### 1. Multi-Tenant Wholesale FMCG ERP Core
> **Domain:** Fast-Moving Consumer Goods Supply Chain, Multi-Warehouse Distribution & Financial Risk Control  
> **Key Architecture:** Multi-tenant PostgreSQL isolation, First-Expired First-Out (FEFO) batch allocation algorithms, driver van-sales stock & cash custody ledger, and automated bounced-check credit locking triggers.  
> **Open Reference Implementation:** [👉 `fmcg-erp-core-architecture`](https://github.com/osama1404/fmcg-erp-core-architecture)

* **Key Breakthrough:** Solved the high-friction settlement gap in regional van-sales operations by enforcing an atomic double-entry custody session model—preventing inventory leakages and cash discrepancies prior to warehouse shift closure. Built compliant UBL 2.1 e-invoicing payload generation with SHA-256 invoice hashing.

---

### 2. Servio — Automotive Workshop & Service Dispatch Platform
> **Domain:** Vehicle Aftermarket Maintenance, Workshop Operations & Telematics Lifecycle  
> **Key Architecture:** Decoupled REST architecture (Node.js/TypeScript + MongoDB) connecting Car Owners with Workshop Service Bay Scheduling, Digital Job Card state machines, and real-time technician labor dispatch.  
> **Google Play Product & Reference Repo:** [👉 `servio-workshop-platform`](https://github.com/osama1404/servio-workshop-platform)

* **Key Breakthrough:** Built an interactive Bay Dispatch conflict-resolution engine that prevents double-booking across physical hydraulic lift bays, digitizing physical work orders into audited state-machine job cards from intake inspection to customer sign-off.

---

### 3. NXN — Smart Logistics & Warehouse Marketplace
> **Domain:** High-Throughput Warehouse Space Brokerage & Cross-Docking Logistics  
> **Key Architecture:** Event-driven multi-depot distribution platform designed for scalable cross-docking, real-time inventory aggregation, and 3PL supplier integration.  
> **Key Capabilities:** Dynamic rate calculation, load consolidation analytics, and automated bill of lading (BOL) generation.
